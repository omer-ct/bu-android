وَلَا أُجْمِرُكُمْ وَإِذَا غِبْتُمْ فِى الْبُعُوْثِ فَأَنَا أَبُوا الْعِيَالْ حَتَّى تَرْجِعُوْ اِلِيْهِمْ مَا مِنْ نَبِيْ إِلَّا وَرَعِى الْغَنَمْ

وَأَنْتُمْ تَقَّحْمُوْنَ فِيْهَا اَهْلُ الْحَلِّ وَالْعَقْدَ Allahعَزّوجَلّ says; فَبِمَا رَحْمَةٍ مِنَ اللَّهِ لِنْتَ لَهُمْ وَلَوْ كُنْتَ فَظًّا غَلِيظَ الْقَلْبِ لَانْفَضُّوا مِنْ حَوْلِكَ

فَاعْفُ عَنْهُمْ وَاسْتَغْفِرْ لَهُمْ وَشَاوِرْهُمْ فِي الْأَمْرِ فَإِذَا عَزَمْتَ فَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُحِبُّ الْمُتَوَكِّلِينَ۝

لَا خَيْرَ فِيْ اَمْرٍاُ بْرِ مَا مِنْ غَيْرِ شُوْرى شَاوِرْ فِيْ أَمْرِكَ مَنْ يَخَافُ الله تَعَالى اِسْمَعْ وَاَطَعْ مِنْ اَصْحَابِيْ اَنَّبِيِّ  صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَشْرِكْمْ فِيْ الْأَمْرِيْ

خَاصَةً مِّنْ كَانَ مِنْهُمْ مِنْ اَهْلِ بَدْرََ وَهُوَ ذُوْا مُجَاهَدَةْ لِلْعَدُّوْ وَمُكَايَدَتَهْ فَإِذَا قَدِمَ عَلَيْكَ فَاسْتَشِرْهُ وَقَرِّبْهُُ

اسْتَشِرْهُ إِذَا جَاءَ نَصْرُ اللَّهِ وَالْفَتْحُ ۝ وَرَأَيْتَ النَّاسَ يَدْخُلُونَ فِي دِينِ اللَّهِ أَفْوَاجًا ۝ فَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ وَاسْتَغْفِرْهُ إِنَّهُ كَانَ تَوَّابًا۝

لَقَدْ قَضَيْتَ بِالْحَقّ إِنِّيْ لَمْ اَبْعَثْ عُمَّالِيْ عَلَيْكُمْ لِيُصِيْبُوْا مِنْ أَبْشَارِكُمْ وَلَا مِنْ اَمْوَالِكُمْ وَإِنَّمَا بَعَثْتَ هُمْ لِيَحْجِزُوْا بَيْنَكُمْ

وَلِيَقْسِمُوْا فَيْءَ كُمْ بَيْنَكُمْ فَمَنْ فَعَلَ فُعِلَ بِهِ غَيْرَ ذَلِكْ فَلْيَقُمْ مُزْمَتَى اسْتَعْبَدْتُمُ النَّاسَ وَقَدْ وَلَدَتْهُمُ أُمَّهَاتُهُمْ اَحْرَارًا؟

وعليكم ألسلام ورحمة الله ربي إني لما أنزلتا إليَّ من خيرٍ فقير من رآني في المنام فقد رآني فإن الشيطان لا يتمثل بي قَدْ اَبَرَّاللهُ يَمِيْنَكْ وَعَظَّمَ أَجْرَكْ

قَدِمَ سُّوْقْ وَطَبٌ مِّن لَّبَنْ وَعَكَّةْ مِنْ سَمَنْ فَابْتَعْتُهَا بِأَرْبَعِيْنْ كَيْفَ يَعْنِنِيْى شَأْنُ الرَّعِيَّةْ، إِذَ لَمْ يَمَسَّنِيْ مَا يَمَسُّهُمْ

مَا مَلَأَ اِبْن آدَمْ وَعَاءً شَرًّا مِّنْ بَطْنِ شِفَآءْ مِنْ كُلِّ دَآءْ شَجَرَةْ مُبَارَكَةْ، زَيْتُوْنَةْ ئْتَدِمُوْا بِالزَّيْتِ وَادَّهِنُوْا بِهْ

قَرْقَرِيْ أَوْلَا تُقَرْقَرِيْ فَوَاللهِ لَا تَأْكُلَ اسَّمْنِ حَتّى يَأْكُلُهُ النَّاسْ دَعْ عَنْكَ هَذَا اِذً أَاتَنَصَّرْ لَايَجْتَمِعْ فِيْهَا دِيْنَانْ

لَا اِكْرَاهَ فِى الدِّيْنَ وَاِنَّا اَعْطَيْنَاهُمُ الْعَهْدْ عَلى اَنْ نُخَلِّيْ بَيْنَهُمْ وَ بَيْنَ كَنَائِسِهِمْ يَقُوْلُوْنَ فِيْهَا مَا بَدَا لَهُمْ

When Abu Bakr As-Siddique رضي الله عنه was feeling, death approaching him he told the Sahabah رضي الله عنهم, I want you, to choose for yourselves a Khalifah(خليفه) after me

your Baiy’ah(بيعة) to me is over, and I am not incharge of your affairs anymore because I am leaving this Duniyah(دُنِيَّا).

And he wanted this affair to be settled before he dies.

So, Sahabah رضي الله عنهم discussed the matter but then they came back to Abu Bakr(ابو بكر) and they said: We trust your advice, so we put it back in your hands you choose for us.

Abu Bakr As-Siddique رضي الله عنه said: Then give me some time to decide.

Abu Bakr As-Siddique رضي الله عنه called Abdur Rahman Ibn Awf(عبدالرحمن ابن عوف) and he asked him, he said: "Inform me about Umar(عُمَر)?" Abdur Rahman Ibn Awf رضي الله عنه said:

"You don’t ask me about anything but you have better knowledge than me." Anything that you ask me about, you know it better than I do.

Abu Bakr As-Siddique رضي الله عنه said: "Even though, I want to hear your opinion?" So, Abdur Rahman Ibn Awf(عبدالرحمن ابن عوف) Said: Umar(عُمَر) is better than what you think of him!

Abu Bakr As-Siddique رضي الله عنه said: Keep this conversation private, he left.

And then Abu Bakr(ابو بكر) invited Uthman Ibn Affan( عثمان ابن عفان), he said: "Tell me about Umar(عُمَر)?" Uthman Ibn Affan(عثمان ابن عفان) said: You know him

basically he said the same thing, "Anything you ask me about, you know it better than I do!" The Sahabah رضي الله عنهم  gave it to Abu Bakr(ابو بكر), that he was most scholarly and knowledgeable among them.

Abu Bakr(ابو بكر) said: I still want to hear your opinion on Umar(عُمَر), he said: I know what I know of Umar(عُمَر), and Allah is my witness.

Is that, his inside is better than his outside and there is none among us, like him.

There is none of us equal to Umar(عُمَر) and what we see of Umar(عُمَر) in the outside and think is good and what is inside is even better.

Abu Bakr As-Siddique رضي الله عنه told Uthman Ibn Affan(عثمان ابن عفان), "If I am going to by pass him, it's not going beyond you!" If I am not going to choose Umar(عُمَر), you will be my next choice.

And then he make Naseeha(نصيحه) with some of the Sahabah رضي الله عنهم for example, Usayed Ibn Khuzair(اُسَيْد اِبن خضير), one of Al-Ansaar(الأنْصَار).

He was asked about Umar(عُمَر).

Abu Bakr As-Siddique رضي الله عنه asked him, Usayed Ibn Khuzair(اُسَيْد اِبن خضير) said: "He is pleased, with what pleases the ummah(امة), and he is displeased with what displeases the Ummah(امة).

And there is none among us, who is more competent, stronger for this affair, then him.

He is the most competent among us, to run this affair.

So, basically all of the Sahabah(صحابه) were giving Umar(عُمَر), the same council but there was the concern of some of the Sahabah(صحابه) for example, Talha(طلحه)

that Umar(عُمَر) is rough and Talha(طلحه) was quite stern in the Naseeha(نصيحه) that he gave Abu Bakr(ابو بكر) he told him, O Abu Bakr(ابو بكر)! How will you answer Allah on the Day of Judgment?

When He tells you, you appointed over the Muslims a man, who is harsh and rough.

Abu Bakr As-Siddique(ابوبكرالصديق) was ill and he was on his bed and he sat up straight and he said: Are you threatening me with Allah?

I will respond to Allah on the Day of Judgment by saying; "I have chosen the best among them to lead them.

he is the best among you.

So, the opinion of Abu Bakr(ابوبكر) was settling on Umar Ibn Al-Khattab رضي الله عنه and he wrote a document of his appointment of Umar(عُمَر).

And he gave that document to Uthman Ibn Affan(عثمان ابن عفان) and he said: "You go and read it in the Masjid(مسجد) and you take a Baiy’ah(بيعة) from the people."

Abu Bakr(ابو بكر) wanted to make sure that this issue is settled before he dies.

So, Uthman(عثمان) goes to the Masjid(مسجد) and, he tells the people, Do you give Baiy’ah(بيعة) to, who is in this document? And they all gave Baiy’ah(بيعة)."

So, Baiy’ah(بيعة) was given to Umar(عُمَر) during the time of Abu Bakr As-Siddique رضي الله عنه before he passes away.

Ibn Masood رضي الله عنه, he says; Ibn Masood(ابن مسعود) says; "The three with the greatest intuitions among mankind are the woman or the wife of Musa(موسى)

THE SECOND SAHIB YUSUF(صاحب يوسُف): When Yusuf(يوسف) was found in the well, he was sold as a slave in Egypt.

Make his stay with us honorable.
