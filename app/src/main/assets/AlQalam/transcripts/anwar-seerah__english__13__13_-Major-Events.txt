The Life Of Muhammadﷺ, The Makkan Period, by Imam Anwar Al_Awlaki, Hamza Bin Abdul Muttalib(حمزه بن عبدالمطلب), he was a hunter.

He would go out and hunt, in the desert and then he would come back and tell other stories about his hunting expeditions.

So, one day while, he was out hunting, Abu Jahal(ابوجَهَل) approached Rasool Allah ﷺ and started cursing Muhammadﷺ.

While the Messenger of Allah was silent, Rasool Allah ﷺ would not respond to the ignorant words.

Allah سبحانه وتعالى told Muhammadﷺ, stay away from the ignorant.

The Muslim should not be sidetracked by trivial issues and should not turn the Da'wah(دعوة) into a personal thing.

One should not take it personally, if the insults are directed towards a person, because that person is calling to Islam, one should not take it personally.

They are not rejecting you, they're rejecting the message that you are presenting them with, that's what they are against.

So, Rasool Allah ﷺ was calm and he did not respond back to Abu Jahal(ابوجَهَل).

Abu Jahal(ابوجَهَل), then threw a stone, a rock at Muhammadﷺ and it hit him in his head and Rasool Allah ﷺ was bleeding.

A slave girl saw that and when Hamza(حمزه) came back from hunting, she went and told him the whole story.

Hamza(حمزه) obviously was very upset, when he heard that this happened to his nephew, Muhammadﷺ.

Even though Hamza(حمزه) was a Mushrik(مشرك), a non believer at that time but because of the relationship between him and Muhammadﷺ he felt that an attack on Muhammad صلوات الله وسلام عليه was attacked on himself.

So, he walked up to Abu Jahal(ابوجَهَل) and Abu Jahal(ابوجَهَل) was sitting with other leaders of Quraish(قُرَيْشْ) right in front of Al-Ka'aba(اَلْكَعْبَه).
