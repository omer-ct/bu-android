The virtues of a Ash-Shuhadaa(ألشُهَدآء).

Ash-Shuhadaa(ألشُهَدآء) are blessed with many virtues from Allah, some of which are, and Ibn Nuhas(إبن نُحاس) I think mentions, how many does he mention?

Are they 19!

He has actually nine. Nine virtues!

The first one is to desire to die again for the sake of Allah.

No one; who enters paradise wants to leave it, even if they are given the world and everything on it, with the exception of the Shaheed(شَهِيد).

He wants to leave paradise come back to this world and be killed in the sake of Allah again.

In Sahih Muslim(صحيح مسلم), the Messenger of Allah says; No one, who enters paradise would want to go back to Earth but the Shaheed(شَهِيد),

he wants to go back to Earth in order to be killed for the sake of Allah 10 times.

That is when he sees what Allah has reserved for the Shuhadaa(شُهَدآء).

Rasool Allah ﷺ himself says in the name of whom Muhammad(محمد) soul in His hand, I wish I could fight in the cause of Allah and then be killed,

and then fight and then be killed and then fight and then be killed.

Number two, forgiveness of all sins, the moment the soul of the Shaheed(شَهِيد) leaves his body, it also leaves all his sins behind.

In Sahih Muslim(صحيح مسلم) Abu Qatadah(أبوقتادة) said: Rasool Allah ﷺ delivered a Khut'ba(خطبة) and he said: The Jihad(جهاد) in the path of Allah and Eeman(ايمان) are the greatest of all deeds.

So a man stood up and said: O Rasool Ullah, if I am killed in the path of Allah, will that forgive my sins?

Rasool Allah ﷺ said: Yes, if you die with steadfastness and sincerity, facing your enemy, and not turning your back to him.

The man asked again, Rasool Allah ﷺ said: Yes!

If you die was steadfastness and sincerity, facing your enemy and not turning your back to him.

And if you are not in debt.

Jibreel(جِبْرِيْل) told me that.

So, the exception here is debt.

Abdullah Bin Amr Bin Al-Aas(عبدالله بن عَمْرُو بِن اَلْعَاص) narrated, The Messenger of Allah said: The Shaheed(شَهِيد) is forgiven everything except debt

and this is in Muslim(ألمسلم) and In Al Hakim(ألحَاكِم) And Ahmad(أحمد).

So what does Al-Qurtubi(ألقرطبي) say about debt?

Al-Qurtubi(ألقرطبي) says: the debt that is intended here, is when the Shaheed(شَهِيد) had the capability of paying it back, but didn't or have the option of writing it in his will, and he didn't.

It would also include money that was borrowed for wasteful reason, and was not returned.

But when the Shaheed(شَهِيد) borrows money due to poverty and dire need, and is then incapable of paying it back, this would not withhold the Shaheed(شَهِيد) from Paradise.

In this case, the Sultan(سلطان) should pay back his debt, if that didn't happen, then Allah Himself will pay it back for him.

As a Rasool Allah ﷺ said: When you borrow and you sincerely want to pay it back, Allah will pay it for you.

And when you borrow money to waste, Allah will waste it.

And that's in Bukhari(بخاريْ).

So this point should be clear about debt.

If a person was in need, serious need, and they borrowed money they went to on Jihad(جهاد).

They couldn't pay it back before they go, and they ended up dying Fee-Sabeel-Lillah(فِي سَبيل الله) that's not gonna withhold them from Jannah(جنة).

But if somebody had enough money to buy a Honda Accord, nevertheless, they wanted to buy a brand new BMW, and they borrowed money for that.

And they didn't pay it back.

That's a debt, that's unforgiving because, it's for a wasteful reason.

The Messenger of Allah said: The Shaheed(شَهِيد) has seven qualities, he would be forgiven with the first drop of his blood.

So just imagine that on the first drop of blood makes a touchdown, all of the sins are gone, Ahmad(أحمد) and Tabrani(طبراني).

When the servant is killed in the cause of Allah, as soon as the first drop of his blood touches the ground, all of his sins are forgiven.

Number three, the angels provide shade for the Shaheed(شَهِيد) with their wings.

Now, Allah Alam(ألله أعلم) if this, Yani(يعني) I believe that this is specific, even though Ibn Nuhas(إبن نُحاس) mentioned it as something for all of the Shuhadaa(شُهَدآء)

but it might be specific to this particular case.

Jabir(جابر) narrated that his father was presented to the Messenger of Allah after his dead body was mutilated.

I wanted to uncover his face but some people told me not to.

We then heard some woman weeping so Rasool Allah ﷺ said: Why are you weeping?

The angels are still providing shade for him up to this moment, Allah Alam(ألله أعلم), this might be a special honor to the father of Jabir Bin Abdullah(جابر بن عبدالله).

Number four martyrdom, guarantees paradise.

Allah says: Indeed Allah has purchased from the believers, their lives and their properties and in exchange for that they will have paradise,

At-Toba(التوبة) Verse 111.

And Allah also says, And those who are killed in the cause of Allah.

Never will He waste their deeds.

He will guide them and amend their condition and admit them to paradise.

Muhammad(محمد), Verse four to six.

Samura Bin Jundab(سمرة بن جندب) narrated, the Messenger of Allah said: I've dreamed last night of two men who ascended with me and took me up to a tree

and we entered the mansion, I've never seen anything more beautiful than it.

They said: This mansion is for the Shuhadaa(شُهَدآء).

Abu Hurairah(ابوهريره) narrated the Messenger of Allah said: Allah laughs at two men, one of them kills the other and they both enter into paradise.

So, they end up having that close bond in Jannah(جنة).

Number five, Their souls are in the inside of green birds in paradise.

Ibn Abbas(إبن عباس) narrated, the Messenger of Allah said: When your brothers were killed at Uhud(أحَد), Allah had their souls in the insides of green birds,

Number six, They are not punished in their graves.

Then what about the one who dies as the Shaheed(شَهِيد).

The interrogation of the grave is meant to test the faith of a person.

Now if the Shaheed(شَهِيد) died fighting for the sake of Allah, with swords flashing above his head, spears and arrows flying next to him, and seeing heads and limbs being chopped off,

whoever sees that and then doesn't retreat, but rather fights until they die.

Giving up their soul for Allah is enough as a test for his faith.

So now you can replace the swords, spears and arrows with fire.

Number seven, The Shaheed(شَهِيد) is spared from the shock of the blow of the horn.

Saeed Ibn Jubair(سعيد إبن جبير) was asked: Who are the ones excluded by Allah  the following verse and the horn will be blown and whoever is in the heavens and whoever is on earth will fall dead except whom Allah wills.

Now there's different views on who these are, The ones who are excluded from this Ayah(آية).

Some say it's talking about Al-Hoor Al-Ain(ألحورالعين) in Jannah(جنة).
